On Inspiration

A Town Between the Sea and Mountains

I was born and raised in a town in rural Japan, surrounded by the sea and mountains. Growing up close to nature, I learned to notice its beauty and how it changes with the seasons and the light.

When I look at nature, I feel more than just what I see. I try to express those feelings and the scenery in my mind through my paintings.
